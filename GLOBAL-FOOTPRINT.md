# Global footprint

Client/location associations supplied by EBA. Cotonou is displayed as a city in Benin; Dubai as a city in the UAE. Omoniyi Dada is a personal brand. Queen Margret uses the supplied spelling and a text treatment pending a verified portrait or logo. Nigeria shows a mix of companies, events, foundations and personal brands.

World geometry: Natural Earth public-domain 1:110m boundaries, via https://github.com/datasets/geo-boundaries-world-110m (countries.geojson). Converted to rounded SVG paths with equirectangular projection; Antarctica omitted. Client locations are navigable by keyboard via map markers and named buttons. The map depicts client relationships, not EBA office locations.
